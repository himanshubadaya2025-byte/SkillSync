How it works

Learning is better together.

Everyone has something to teach and something to learn. SkillSync helps you connect the two in a simple way.

1

Sign up and tell the community which skills you can teach and which ones you want to learn.

2

3

Exchange Knowledge

Connect with your partner, complete your learning session, and participate in a community of learners.

Knowledge is valuable. Share it.

SkillSync uses a simple Skill Credit system to support learning exchanges. New students receive two starter credits. When an exchange is completed, one credit moves from the learner to the student who taught the skill.
